KeyGlo

Artist-to-beat key matching. KeyGlo detects your beat's key, scale, BPM and tuning, profiles your artist's vocal range, scores how a hook fits, recommends a transposition, and tunes your 808s and samples to the track.

Free, local, and unlocked

  • No trial timer, no locked features, no watermark
  • No account and no internet connection required to use it
  • All analysis runs on your machine — nothing is uploaded

This installer places

  • VST3 in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • Standalone app in /Applications

You can choose which of the three to install on the next screens. Installing to the system folders requires your administrator password.

Requires

  • macOS 11 Big Sur or later
  • Apple Silicon or Intel — this is a universal build
