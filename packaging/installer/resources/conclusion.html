Getting started

  • Drop a beat on the Beat Analysis panel — KeyGlo reads its key, scale, BPM and tuning. Or just play your session and press the refresh arrow to analyse what it hears.
  • Run the range test in Artist Range to build a vocal profile, then Save Profile to keep it.
  • Sing over the beat and the fit scores light up, with a recommended transposition you can audition.
  • Drop an 808 or sample on the 808 / Sample Tune panel for its true sustain note and a tuning recommendation.

If your DAW does not list it

Logic and GarageBand scan Audio Units on launch — quit and reopen them. Most VST3 hosts have a "rescan plug-ins" button in their preferences.

KeyGlo is free and stays free: no trial, no locked panels, no account.
