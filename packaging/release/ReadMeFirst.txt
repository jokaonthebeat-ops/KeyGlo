Artist-to-Beat Key Matching
Diamond Loopz

===============================================================
INSTALLING
===============================================================

Open "Install KeyGlo.pkg" and follow the prompts. You can choose
which formats to install:

  VST3       -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  Audio Unit -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  Standalone -> /Applications

Installing to the system plug-in folders requires your
administrator password.

Requires macOS 11 Big Sur or later. Universal: runs natively on
both Apple Silicon and Intel.

===============================================================
IF YOUR DAW DOES NOT LIST IT
===============================================================

Logic Pro and GarageBand scan Audio Units when they launch --
quit and reopen them after installing.

Most VST3 hosts (Ableton Live, FL Studio, Studio One, REAPER,
Cubase) have a "rescan plug-ins" button in their preferences.

KeyGlo is an audio EFFECT, not an instrument. Look for it under
your host's effect list, filed under Diamond Loopz.

===============================================================
GETTING STARTED
===============================================================

1. BEAT
   Drag a beat onto the Beat Analysis panel and KeyGlo reads its
   key, scale, BPM and tuning offset. Or just play your session
   and press the small refresh arrow to analyse what it hears.

   If the material is pure drums or noise, KeyGlo says NO
   RELIABLE KEY rather than inventing one.

2. ARTIST
   Press START RANGE TEST and sing the four prompts: lowest
   comfortable note, centre, highest comfortable, and optionally
   falsetto. Take your time -- each step waits for you. Then
   SAVE PROFILE to keep that artist's range.

3. FIT
   Sing over the beat. ARTIST FIT, RANGE FIT and HOOK MATCH
   score how the hook sits in that artist's voice against that
   beat, and KeyGlo recommends a transposition.

   The recommendation is shown as a gold BEST badge. Nothing is
   applied until you click a transpose button -- KeyGlo never
   pitch-shifts your track on its own.

4. 808s AND SAMPLES
   Drop an 808 or one-shot on the 808 / Sample Tune panel for
   its true sustain note, the cents it is off, and a tuning
   recommendation matched to your beat's scale.

   SOLO auditions the tuned version. APPLY TUNE writes a tuned
   copy next to the original file -- your source is never
   modified.

5. AUTO-TUNE SETUP
   Shows the key, scale and allowed notes to enter into your
   pitch-correction plug-in. COPY SCALE copies the setup as
   text; DRAG the same button into your DAW to drop a one-octave
   MIDI scale file.

===============================================================
YOUR DATA
===============================================================

Everything runs locally on your machine. KeyGlo does not upload
your audio, require an account, or contact any server.

Artist profiles are small files in
~/Library/Application Support/Diamond Loopz/KeyGlo/
containing vocal range settings only -- never recorded audio.
