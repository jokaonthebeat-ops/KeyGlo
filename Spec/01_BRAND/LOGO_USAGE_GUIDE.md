# KeyGlo Logo Usage Guide

Use the supplied path-converted SVG or transparent PNG. Do not redraw the name with a system font inside the plugin. The SVG contains vector glyph paths and does not require or distribute font files.

## Plugin header

- Base-canvas bounds: **x=35, y=17, w=250, h=58**.
- Use `keyglo_header_logo_250x58.png` at 1× and `keyglo_header_logo_500x116.png` at 2×.
- Preserve transparent margins and aspect ratio.

## Marketing

- Use `keyglo_premium_logo_2048w.png` or the SVG master.
- The circular mark may be used alone for icons, splash screens, installers, and favicons.
- Do not place the logo on a permanent dark rectangle. It is supplied with transparency.
- Do not recolor it away from the cyan/violet identity without creating an approved theme variant.
